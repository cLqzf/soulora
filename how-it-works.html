How it works

A closer look at Soulora.

An AI companion concept for everyday conversation, built around listening, memory with permission, and clear boundaries. Here is what you can explore today.

What you can try in the preview

The homepage contains a set of local, interactive demos. You can explore them without signing up. They illustrate a possible experience; they do not connect you to an AI companion.

Explore a conversation
Open Listen and choose a suggested reply or enter a short demo message. The responses are scripted, not generated by an AI model. Use an everyday example rather than private information.
Try a memory decision
Open Memory to allow, edit, or decline the example suggestion. These controls demonstrate a decision flow. They do not create a lasting memory or update a user account.
Set the pace
Try the optional 30-second breathing timer, switch avatar styles, or explore the visual voice player. Reloading the page resets the demo choices.

The idea behind memory by consent

A useful companion might remember that you prefer a little quiet before a busy day. That does not mean every conversation should become a permanent record. Soulora's design separates the current conversation from information you choose to keep.

The proposed flow is to show a memory suggestion, explain its purpose, and let you decide whether it belongs in long-term memory. Viewing, changing, and deleting saved information are part of that design goal.

What the preview actually does: the controls change the current demo state. They do not save information long term. A future product's storage, retention, and deletion rules have not been published.

When evaluating a released product, check whether deleting chat history also deletes its memories. They may be separate controls. Our guide to evaluating AI memory explains the questions to ask.

Voice and avatars are a product direction

The voice player shows a sample transcript, waveform, and playback progress. No real audio is played or uploaded. It does not listen through your microphone.

The avatar selector changes the displayed character image, tone, and example line. Real-time avatar conversation is a planned integration, not a shipped capability. The homepage identifies the visual material and links to its technology source.

Explore the avatar concept. Before choosing any voice companion, test the language, speaking pace, interruptions, and microphone controls that matter to you.

Before you explore

Is Soulora an AI friend or a roleplay platform?

The current concept focuses on everyday conversation, reflection, and companionship. The avatar examples show different styles of presence; they are not a live catalogue of roleplay characters.

Does the preview remember me after I leave?

No. The demos do not create an account or long-term memory. Refreshing the page resets their state.

Can I register or download Soulora?

Not yet. The homepage's early-access flow is a transparent preview, not an active registration or waitlist.

Is Soulora a substitute for therapy?

No. This is a concept for everyday conversation, not diagnosis, treatment, or crisis support. Health and crisis situations call for qualified real-world support.
