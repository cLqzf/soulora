04 TRUST BY DESIGN

Closeness should never cost you control.

Companionship worthy of trust must be clear about what it is, what it remembers, and how you can withdraw permission at any time.

  • Always clearly AINever impersonates a person or manufactures a false human relationship.
  • Memory you can see, edit, and deleteLong-term content should never become an unexplained black box.
  • Not a substitute for professional careHealth and crisis situations should lead to qualified real-world support.

06 QUESTIONS

Before we get closer,
let's make the boundaries clear.

More questions? Before launch, we'll publish complete privacy, terms of service, and data-processing information.

Is Soulora a real person?

No. Soulora is an AI companion product concept. It clearly identifies itself as AI, never pretends to be human, and does not replace real-world relationships.

Will it automatically remember everything I say?

The design goal is to separate the current conversation from long-term memory. Anything saved for the long term must explain its purpose and receive your permission first.

Can it replace therapy or medical care?

No. Soulora is a concept for everyday conversation and emotional support—not diagnosis or treatment. In a crisis or health situation, contact qualified real-world support.

Can I download it now?

Not yet. This website presents an interactive product concept. Launch timing, supported platforms, and a real application channel will be announced when they are ready.
