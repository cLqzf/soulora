Website privacy

Privacy notice for the Soulora website.

This notice describes the current concept website at soulora.ai. Soulora is still in development and does not yet provide accounts, AI chat, downloads, or a live waitlist.

Effective and updated 22 September 2026

Interim website notice

The public legal operator, jurisdiction, and product-specific data practices have not been finalized. This notice records the website's current, limited behavior and must be replaced or expanded before a live service collects product data.

What this notice covers

This notice covers visits to the public Soulora website and messages voluntarily sent to support@soulora.com. It does not describe an unreleased Soulora app or AI companion service.

The website is an English-language concept preview. English is also the intended website language for Singapore at this stage. That does not mean the future product already supports English voice or chat in any particular market.

Information handled by the current website

Technical request data

When a browser requests the site, the hosting and delivery infrastructure may process standard connection information such as IP address, request time, requested URL, browser or device details, referring page, and security signals. This is inherent in serving and protecting a website.

Messages you send

If you email support, the message, sender address, attachments, and related correspondence are processed so the request can be answered. Do not send passwords, government identifiers, medical records, financial information, or other sensitive material.

Local preview choices

The scripted conversation, avatar selector, memory example, breathing timer, and feedback-flow preview run in the current browser page. The published site does not use cookies, local storage, session storage, or an active form endpoint to retain those choices.

What the site does not collect today

  • No Soulora user account or profile exists on this website.
  • The conversation examples are scripted and are not sent to an AI model.
  • The visual voice preview does not access the microphone or upload audio.
  • The feedback preview has no configured submission endpoint; entries are not sent or saved by Soulora.
  • No advertising, analytics, or Vercel Speed Insights script is currently installed in the published source.

Why current website data may be used

Technical request data may be used to deliver the site, maintain reliability, investigate errors, prevent abuse, and protect the site. Support correspondence is used to answer the request, keep necessary records of the conversation, and address related security or legal obligations.

The current website does not use visitor information for personalized advertising or AI model training. If measurement tools or a live product are introduced, this notice will be updated before those practices begin.

Hosting and service providers

The website is hosted and delivered through Vercel. Vercel may process technical request data as a hosting, content-delivery, and security provider under its own infrastructure and policies. You can review Vercel's privacy policy for information about its practices.

The provider for support@soulora.com and its retention settings have not been documented publicly yet. This information remains a launch requirement.

Retention, access, deletion, and export

The site source does not create Soulora accounts or store preview entries. Hosting logs and support email records may exist outside the site source. Their precise retention periods have not yet been published.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your support email. Where applicable, you may also request a portable copy or object to processing. Requests are handled subject to identity verification, technical availability, and applicable legal requirements.

Follow the steps on the data requests page. Because the public operator and jurisdiction are still pending, jurisdiction-specific rights and response periods will be added before product launch.

Changes and contact

This notice will change when analytics, registration, downloads, AI chat, memory, voice, payments, or other product functions are introduced. The effective date above will be updated when the text or practices materially change.

Privacy questions can be sent to support@soulora.com. The public legal entity, address, jurisdiction, privacy owner, and independent legal reviewer are not yet available and are recorded as unresolved launch items.

Need to make a data request?

The current site has no account data, but you can ask about support correspondence or technical records associated with you.
